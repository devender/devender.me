Let me take you on a journey.

No, no need to pack. It's just a mental journey.

What do you love?

Ice cream? Horror movies? Classical music?

Pick anything. Heck, you can even add your own to the list.

For the sake of this journey, let's say you love horror movies.

And I mean *really* love them.

You're an aficionado.

You know the directors, the writers, the composers. You could spend hours talking about the little nuances that most people miss but that make an enormous difference.

That seemingly insignificant character in the background wearing period-accurate clothing. That subtle change in the music that tells you something is about to happen. That seemingly meaningless conversation that suddenly makes sense an hour later.

You appreciate the craftsmanship. The thought. The effort that went into making something special.

There is something deeply satisfying about watching all those little pieces come together.

Now, imagine you're sitting in a theater.

The lights are dim. The movie is about 25 minutes in.

Everything is building up beautifully.

You've noticed something interesting about one of the characters. You have a theory about where the story is going. You're eager to see if you're right.

You're completely immersed.

The character slowly reaches for a door handle...

**BHAM!**

Someone grabs you, yanks you out of your seat, drags you out of the theater, and shoves you into another one.

What the hell just happened?

You sit there, bewildered.

What was behind that door?

You look around, trying to get your bearings.

Ah, another horror movie.

Well, okay.

A few minutes pass. Something on the screen catches your attention.

Hmm... that's interesting.

A few more minutes go by, and you begin to appreciate what this director is trying to do.

You've almost forgotten about the previous movie.

You're invested again.

Something is about to be revealed. You lean forward in your seat...

**BHAM!**

You've got to be kidding me.

You're yanked out and shoved into yet another theater.

This time, you're angry.

You were enjoying the first movie. You made an effort to get into the second one. And just when you started enjoying that one...

You take a deep breath.

Fine.

You're here now. Might as well make the most of it.

You sit down, collect yourself, and slowly begin paying attention.

Oh, I know this actor.

Hmm... the music isn't bad. Could use some work, though.

Ah, that was clever.

Didn't see that twist coming.

For a moment, you're reminded of why you love horror movies.

**BHAM!**

Another theater.

You sit down.

But this time, something is different.

You find yourself looking at the entrance every few minutes.

Not the screen.

The entrance.

You wonder how long you have before someone comes to yank you out again.

You notice something interesting in the movie, but you don't give it much thought.

Why bother?

You're probably never going to find out what it means anyway.

You stop forming theories.

You stop wondering what happens next.

You stop appreciating the little details.

Not because the movie is bad.

Not because you've suddenly lost your love for horror movies.

But because every time you allow yourself to become invested, something comes along and takes it away.

So you lean back in your seat.

And close your eyes.

The movie continues to play.

**BHAM!**
